## Sorting tasks

Use `sort` to group and reorder tasks. Deadlines are ordered by their `/by`
date, events by their `/from` date, and todos alphabetically. Tasks with dates
that Eli cannot parse appear after dated tasks and are ordered alphabetically.

The sorted order is saved and remains in effect the next time Eli starts.
